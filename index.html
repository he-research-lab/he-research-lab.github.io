He Group贺梦飞课题组

We study how liquids and flexible materials flow, deform, and interact. Our interests range from the thin liquid films left behind by moving contact lines to the wrinkles of elastic sheets wrapped around curved surfaces and the opening and closing of flexible valves. Through experiments, we explore how geometry and confinement give rise to unexpected shapes and motions. Our goal is to uncover the physical principles behind these phenomena and use them to guide new ways of controlling shape and flow.

Research

Slender Fluid Structures薄层流体力学

Investigating capillary-driven phenomena, droplet dynamics, and the physics of rapid wetting processes on a substrate

Slender Elastic Structures薄膜弹性力学

Studying the mechanical behavior of ultrathin elastic filaments and membranes, including buckling, wrinkling, and shape selection.

Internal Physiological Flows生理流动与瓣膜力学

Exploring flow-induced elastic instabilities in internal physiological flows, with implications for biological and biomedical systems.

Other Soft Matter Phenomena软物质与复杂流体界面

Broader investigations into soft matter physics, including pattern formation, complex instabilities, and coupled phenomena.
